Frequently Asked Questions

Is PulseNet really free?

Yes, PulseNet is open-source. You only pay for your own infrastructure, which usually costs a fraction of a cent per thousand events.

Do I need a cookie consent banner?

No. PulseNet does not use cookies, local storage, or fingerprinting to track users, making it 100% GDPR compliant out of the box.

How does it compare to Mixpanel?

PulseNet is the best alternative to Mixpanel for teams that prioritize data privacy, ownership, and budget, without sacrificing core event analytics.

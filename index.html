Zero-Cookie Architecture

Laplace Noise Engine

Why Differential Privacy?

Differential privacy provides a mathematical framework for quantifying and managing privacy risks. By adding calibrated Laplace noise to analytics streams, it ensures that the output of an analysis does not significantly change whether any specific individual's data is included or not. This protects against linkage attacks and deanonymization, making it the gold standard for compliance (GDPR, CCPA) while preserving high-fidelity statistical aggregates for business intelligence.
